IO Roman Senate rome_io_senate ▶
Overview
DescriptionThe restored Senate of Rome serves as both a governing institution and an international organization, projecting Roman authority and legal tradition across the civilized world. Member states gain access to Roman law, trade agreements, and mutual defense obligations.
CreatedOn Roman Empire formation. Path A: Senate Alignment 50. Path B: Alignment 10 (diminished) or 0 (abolished). Path C: Alignment 80 (sovereign).
Membership
LeaderRoman Empire (permanent). Emperor presides over all sessions.
MembersAny nation that has adopted the Codex Civilis law, OR has 100+ opinion with Rome and shares religion. Max 12 members.
JoiningDecision: "Petition the Senate" -- requires 80+ opinion, same religion group, and Roman Empire approval. Costs 200 prestige. Rome can invite nations with 150+ opinion.
Leaving-100 Roman opinion, -20 prestige, lose all Senate benefits. Expelled if at war with Rome or alignment drops below 10.
Alignment & Voting
AlignmentScale 0 (rubber stamp) to 100 (sovereign). Determines Senate's independence and power. Drifts toward 50 over time unless pushed by events or decisions.
PoliciesVoted every 10 years by all members. Each policy lasts until the next vote.
Vote: OversightSenatorial Oversight (req. alignment 60+): All members -5% unrest, +5% stability. Emperor loses 10% ruler power.
Vote: ImperialImperial Prerogative (alignment <40): Emperor +15% tax, +10% gov cap. Members lose voting rights for 10yr.
Vote: ProvincialProvincial Autonomy (alignment 40-60): Each member +5% local autonomy reduction, +5% trade. Balanced compromise.
Vote: EmergencyPermanent Emergency (Path B only): All power to Emperor. Senate alignment locked at 0. Members become subjects. Cannot be reversed without civil war.
Benefits
TradeAll members: +10% trade with other members, -25% trade agreement cost.
LegalMembers with Codex Civilis: +15 mutual opinion, -10% unrest in shared-culture provinces.
DefenseMembers may call Senate to condemn aggressors: if passed, all members get -50% AE for wars against the condemned nation for 10yr.
PrestigeRome: +1 prestige/yr per member. Members: +0.5 prestige/yr for belonging.
